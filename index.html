Our Story

Positioned in Hornstull, in the heart of Södermalm, R&Co Blommor began with a simple belief: flowers shouldn't just decorate a space, they should carry feeling. What started as an affection for natural textures and seasonal botanicals has grown into our creative studio on Långholmsgatan.

We believe in the tactile art of floristry, pairing deliberate color palettes with unexpected seasonal foliage, letting organic shapes lead the design, and treating every stem as a distinct piece of character. For us, floristry is about genuine craftsmanship, personal touch, and bringing the quiet beauty of nature into the heart of the city.
